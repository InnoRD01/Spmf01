The programme

Small batches. Real skills. A path to work.

IIL is a purpose-driven programme for individuals, especially those with limited digital access, who want practical skills for today's workplace.

Each batch runs for 10 days with just 10 participants, and we run two batches a month. We partner with experienced educators to keep learning supportive, personal and high-impact.

Beyond the classroom, IIL connects sincere learners to real employment opportunities, helping to address unemployment and financial hardship in our community.

What you learn

Everyday tools, taught hands-on

  • Gmail

    Set up and manage a professional email account, and write clear messages.

  • Google Suite

    Docs, Sheets, Drive and more, for work, study and applications.

  • Canva

    Design posters, CVs and social posts that look professional.

  • AI tools

    Use AI assistants responsibly to learn, write and work faster.

  • Typing

    Build speed and accuracy on the keyboard.

  • English communication

    Speak and write with confidence, including in interviews.

Alongside the tools, every batch builds confidence, curiosity and a growth mindset.
